This is everything Marmalade on the site (almost), and where each thing came from/was inspired by. Like the original Marmalade, which then I made into the PNG tuber model, which then I made into pixel art, that Nycto then turned into actual bead art! 

Similar to how you fork a project on GitHub, I wanted to have a nice pretty way to see how each piece of Marmalade art grew out of something else, and what inspired it. A family tree was *the* way to show that, and also as a thank you to [everyone who inspired me](#inspirations).

Under the grass are the drawings from before she even had a name (or more like, she was Gothfield), and the actual LoRA, as the seed. And at the very, very bottom, the Gothfield joke that started it all. They all kinda come together in [the origin](/lab/marmalade-origin/), and everything else grows out of that.

If there is a green eye, that is the CC0 stamp (so you can use it!). A dashed outline means someone else made it, so it's theirs (unless it has the eye too), and the tape under a card is any inspiration from outside the tree.
